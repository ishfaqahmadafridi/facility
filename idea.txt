**✅ FINAL COMPLETE PROJECT DOCUMENT** **App Name:** KamKaro (Recommended) **Alternative Names:** Haazir, Mistri Connect, PakGig, Chowk Connect **Document Version:** 2.2 (Final Consolidated) **Date:** May 2026 **Type:** Full Professional Specification Document (Ready to copy into Microsoft Word)---### Executive Summary**KamKaro** is Pakistan’s all-in-one hyper-local on-demand services and gig economy mobile app. It connects customers with verified service providers for home repairs, daily labour, home healthcare (nurses), beauty & tailoring, light rides & transport, and more.**Core Innovation**: Single account with **clear dual-mode switching** (Customer Mode ↔ Provider Mode). Only **one mode is active at a time**. A plumber can work as a service provider in the morning and book a rider or nurse as a customer in the evening by explicitly switching modes.**Platforms**: Android + iOS (Flutter – single codebase) **Target Cities (Phase 1)**: Lahore or Karachi **Rating**: 8.7/10 **Monetization**: 10-15% commission, provider subscriptions, featured listings, partnerships.---### 1. Problem Statement & Opportunity- Middlemen take huge cuts from daily wage workers.- Lack of trust, verification, and quality control.- Daily wage workers (plumbers, painters, nurses, mazdoor) struggle to find consistent work.- Customers face difficulty finding reliable, fairly priced services.- Women and low-literacy users need safe, simple, voice-supported solutions.- Existing apps are fragmented (repair-focused or ride-only like inDrive).**Opportunity**: Build one trusted super app that combines everything with strong verification, safety, and dual-role flexibility.---### 2. User Roles & Dual Mode System (Fully Clarified)Every user creates **one single account** but operates in only **one mode at a time**.- **Provider Mode**: User works and earns (e.g., as Plumber, Nurse, Painter, Rider, Labour, etc.).- **Customer Mode**: User hires services, books rides, posts jobs, etc.**Key Rule**: The user cannot use Provider features and Customer features at the same time. They must **explicitly switch modes**.**How Mode Switching Works**:- Bottom navigation: **Home | Search | Post Job | Messages | Profile**- Icon: 👤 ↔ 🛠️- On switching, a smooth animation occurs and the entire Home screen refreshes according to the new mode.- Top header always shows current mode clearly, e.g., **“Plumber Mode – Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi”** or **“Customer Mode – Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi”**.**Real-Life Example (Plumber User)**:1. User registers and selects **Plumber** → Starts in **Provider Mode**.2. Morning: Home screen shows plumbing jobs near him. He accepts jobs and earns money.3. Evening: He needs a bike rider or a nurse for his mother.4. He goes to Profile → taps **“Switch to Customer Mode”**.5. App refreshes → Now in Customer Mode.6. Home screen now shows customer categories (Rides, Healthcare, etc.).7. He books a rider or nurse easily.8. Later he can switch back to Plumber Mode anytime.This design keeps the app simple, clean, and focused.---### 3.
